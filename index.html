More than a catch

A little more at home, wherever we cast.

以钓会友，在异乡找到熟悉的温暖。

Moving to a new country means discovering a lot of things from scratch. Fishing is one way to slow down, explore the lakes and rivers around us, and meet people who share a familiar love of the outdoors.

We’re a community for Chinese anglers across Canada: beginners learning their first knots, experienced hands sharing what they know, and families making room for one more fishing story.

Our kind of day

There’s room on the shore for everyone.
